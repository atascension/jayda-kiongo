To my Mom,

For all the times that I forgot to thank you,

for all the special, little things you do.

For all the words that sometimes go

unspoken, I need to say,

I love you, Mom...I do.

I love you for the way you stop and listen,

and for your kind support throughout

the years, for teaching me

the meaning of compassion,

and sharing in my

triumphs and my tears.

And, if at times, I may have seemed

ungrateful, I want to say, I truly hope you see,

that nothing you have done has been forgotten,

and day by day you mean more to me.
